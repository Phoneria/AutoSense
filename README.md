# AutoSense — AI-Powered Used Car Intelligence for Dealers

> Live & Active | Real Users | Built for Turkish Auto Market

AutoSense is an AI agent platform built for used car dealers. It continuously scrapes Arabam.com, applies intelligent filtering far beyond what the platform natively offers, and enables automated AI-driven communication workflows — giving dealers a serious competitive edge in a fast-moving market.

---

## The Problem

Used car dealers spend hours manually browsing Arabam.com, filtering through irrelevant listings, and reaching out to sellers one by one. By the time they act, the best deals are gone.

AutoSense automates the entire pipeline — from discovery to outreach.

---

## What AutoSense Does

### Intelligent Listing Scraper
- Continuously monitors Arabam.com for new listings in real-time
- Goes far beyond the platform's native filters
- Detects underpriced listings, motivated sellers, and high-margin opportunities using AI scoring

### AI-Powered Filtering Engine
- Multi-dimensional filtering: price anomalies, mileage vs. age analysis, listing quality scoring, seller behavior patterns
- Custom dealer profiles — each dealer sets their own criteria and AutoSense learns their preferences over time
- Instant alerts when a high-value listing matches a dealer's profile

### Automated Messaging Infrastructure
- AI agent handles initial outreach to sellers on behalf of dealers
- Personalized, natural-sounding messages that don't feel automated
- Conversation management — tracks responses, flags interested sellers, escalates to dealer when human touch is needed

### Dealer Dashboard
- Clean overview of monitored listings, outreach status, and response rates
- Full conversation history per listing
- Performance analytics: how many listings found, contacted, converted
